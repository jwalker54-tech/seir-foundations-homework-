In previous jobs I have overseen capacity management which would monitor the CPU, memory, and disk utilization of servers. This information is important because it can tell you the 'health' of the server. If CPU is high then it could slow down the machine and even processes to hang. High memory can do the same and high disk space Input/Output can show you that you may need faster storage or even more of it. Slowness can also be attributed to potential hardware failure. Realistically you want to see a trend to know if it is problem and analyze what else is happening at the time of any spikes.
